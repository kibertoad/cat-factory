The project takeover gains a Stages tab, opened first for a staged project: each stage of the pinned definition with its status, what it waits on, the task working on it, whether it is stale, and the report on its newest result against its acceptance criteria. A person starts a ready stage and accepts a result at its checkpoint there, settling a decisions stage item by item as it is accepted.
