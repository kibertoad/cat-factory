An inbox card that asks a person for something now carries its verb as the primary button ("Answer" for a parked decision, "Review", "Test", "Triage" and so on). The button opens the surface the card's title already opened, and "Mark read" becomes a secondary button. Cards whose own action is already the verb (Merge, Retry run, Acknowledge) are unchanged.
