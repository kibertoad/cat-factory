Narrow the pipeline builder's saved-pipeline library on the purpose being edited

The purpose dial narrowed the agent palette beside it and gated the save, but the library in the
third column listed the whole workspace catalog whatever the draft was for. `pipelineMatchesPurpose`
is the membership predicate, applied through `narrowPipelineLibrary` alongside the existing label
and archive filters, with a "n hidden for this purpose" hint so a narrowed list never reads as the
whole one. A pipeline carrying no purpose stays listed at every purpose.
